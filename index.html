A LETTER FOR YOU

Dear Sneha,

Nine years ago, I didn't know that one person could become such a beautiful part of my life.

But then you came into my life. Slowly, you became more than just someone I loved. You became my comfort, my happiness, my favorite person, and my home.

We have had beautiful days. We have had difficult days. We have had silly arguments, countless laughs, misunderstandings, apologies and memories that only the two of us understand.

And through everything, there is still one person I want beside me.

You.

I'm sorry for every moment when I hurt you, disappointed you, or made you feel less loved than you deserve.

I know that love isn't about having a perfect relationship. It's about two imperfect people choosing to stay, understand, forgive, grow, and love each other again every single day.

And if life gives me the choice all over again...

I would still choose you.

I want to be there for your happiest days. I want to hold your hand through the difficult ones. I want to make more memories with you. I want to grow old with you.

Nine years behind us. A lifetime ahead of us.

I love you, Sneha.

Today. Tomorrow. And every day after that.

Forever yours, Pawan ❤️
